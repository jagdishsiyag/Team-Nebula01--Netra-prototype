Netra prototype · SIH26038 · Team Nebula — generated client-side, not a diagnostic device.
Prototype for demonstration purposes only — analysis runs entirely in your browser on pixel colour and contrast, as a stand-in for the trained deep-learning model (ResNet/EfficientNet + Grad-CAM) described in the technical approach. The heatmap is generated from these same pixel statistics, and the "AI explanation" text is produced by a language model summarising the computed findings — neither is a real diagnostic output. Not a diagnostic device.
